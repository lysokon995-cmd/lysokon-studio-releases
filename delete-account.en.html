Account deletion

Version 2026-10-31.1. Contact: lysokon995@gmail.com.

You can start deletion of the planner account and its associated records in the app: Settings → Account → Delete planner account. You must sign in again to the same Google account. The Google account itself is not deleted. If the server rules have not yet been updated, the app stops with a technical code; use the address below to request deletion. Export any records you need before deleting.

If the application has been uninstalled or you cannot sign in, send an account deletion request by email. You do not need to install another application or use another account. State that you want to delete your Lysokon Studio account. Do not send passwords, API keys, tokens, task contents or identity documents. Support will verify account ownership proportionately and confirm the result or explain a delay/refusal provided for by law.

The planner sign-in record and associated synchronized tasks, conversations, memory and planner name are deleted. Minimal service markers for disabling transfers, without record text, may remain to prevent restoration by an old device; their retention period and legal basis are clarified upon request. Independent original local records, exported files, offline copies and information already sent to a separate AI service do not disappear automatically: they require the corresponding cleanup on devices or with the provider.
