Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-04.2. Contact: lysokon995@gmail.com.

Data used

The planner uses the tasks, dates, statuses and settings you enter. You choose the name and logo. Device assessment reads the CPU type and RAM amount to recommend a model; it does not collect files, contacts or a list of apps. Conversation history and confirmed memory are retained only by a separate choice. Google/Firebase Analytics SDKs and advertising identifiers are not integrated. Optional assistant-improvement examples and technical reports are described separately below.

Legal bases and separate permissions

Local features carry out your selected actions on entered records and settings to perform the agreement for use of the application. Where the law requires separate consent for additional processing, it is requested before the relevant feature. An account, selected synchronization categories, cloud AI, microphone access and improvement participation are not enabled by accepting the license once. Declining an optional feature does not block the basic planner.

Account and synchronization

Current synchronization uses Firebase Firestore in São Paulo. Saved ordinary chats belong to the account’s permitted history and are sent manually with Synchronize. On another device, use the same button after signing into the same account and allowing retained history. Turning history off stops transfer and clears device queue copies; explicit account-history deletion also removes server conversations. Firebase rules restrict access to the account owner. The project administrator and Google have technical access; end-to-end encryption is not implemented. Google Drive is disabled in this release pending Google’s written use-case decision. The following Drive description applies only if it is enabled later with a separate choice. Google sign-in uses the account identifier, account name/address for display and session tokens. Signing in alone does not enable content transfer. Google Drive is connected by a separate explicit action and Google authorization for the hidden app folder. Sync covers tasks, the title, permitted retained task-assistant command history, confirmed memory and saved ordinary conversations. Earlier history and memory refusals remain until the corresponding permission is separately changed. Phones also require an allowed offline copy to retain chats. Connecting Drive does not authorize improvement examples, technical reports or text to be sent to cloud AI. Logos, models, AI keys, voice recordings, drafts and device settings do not sync. The app does not receive your Google password. Firebase Authentication continues to process sign-in data separately in the USA. Earlier Firestore records in São Paulo remain until the available local copy has been verifiably transferred to Drive and are not automatically deleted afterwards. Verifying a Drive write and readback does not itself establish that all records have been received from the previous server; preserve the original copies if that download is incomplete. After migration, a Drive sign-in error does not enable content transfer back to Firebase. Previous-message context requires memory permission and, for cloud use, history permission for the selected service. Ordinary chat does not receive tasks or confirmed rules and cannot change planner records. Saving or continuing chats does not enable model training or example uploads.

Cloud AI and voice

The selected AI receives your command and only context that you separately allow. Before sending, you select a specific service and model. Planner permissions do not change the service's contract, retention periods or account settings. Ordinary free Google APIs are unsuitable for personal, sensitive or confidential commands; real use is blocked without renewed confirmation of permitted use and a suitable billing project. Groq may retain abuse/reliability logs, generally for up to 30 days or longer where legally required, unless the relevant ZDR setting is enabled in the account. OpenRouter uses separate no-training and ZDR filters; these can reduce model availability and do not override legal exceptions, the service's own settings or separate safety retention. If a strict route is unavailable, a less private route is not selected automatically. Speech recognition can use operating-system/browser services and the network according to the platform and permissions; the application does not save audio recordings. Background activation and manual dictation are controlled separately.

Accepting the terms, history, memory, sign-in and cloud AI do not replace it; the old installer checkbox does not authorize uploads. Each example is sent with a separate button: a user-reviewed, de-identified command/expected-result pair, model, language and date. Full task lists, history, audio, keys and files are not sent. You can decline without limiting core features, withdraw consent and delete examples in settings. If the network disconnects, retry deletion: success is confirmed by checking that the collection is empty. A minimal disabled consent record remains to protect against resubmission. Sending an example does not change model weights; new recipients, purposes or data categories require new consent. Do not send personal data or secrets: automated checking does not replace human review. Examples are linked to your account UID for withdrawal and deletion. This is pseudonymized data, not a fully anonymous submission. Manual example submission remains a separate Firebase feature and does not read Drive content. Drive records are not automatically included in examples.

Technical connections and security

Update checks contact GitHub, model downloads contact the weight source, and selected cloud features contact the relevant service. Those services may see your IP address and technical connection metadata. Tasks are not included in update checks or installers. Keys are kept in the Mac/Windows system secure store; on a phone they exist only in the current session. Cloud-record access is restricted by account. No application guarantees absolute security.

Retention, withdrawal and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Turning sync off stops transfer but does not remove server records. Clearing cloud data and deleting the planner account are different actions. Full account deletion requires identity confirmation, successful content cleanup, deletion of the Firebase Authentication record and the account’s local profile on the device initiating deletion; it does not delete your Google account. Other installations may retain their own local copies even after receiving the server deletion marker; where needed, clear those copies separately on those devices. Before full deletion, the server block against re-enabling sync is checked. If strengthened server rules have not yet been applied, deletion stops with a technical code and the sign-in record is not reported as deleted. An error is not reported as completion. Independent original local records and files you export elsewhere are not deleted. Offline devices and data already sent to providers may require separate cleanup. Minimal service markers for disabling/deletion without task text remain to prevent restoration by stale devices; request their removal through support, subject to review of active sessions and the lawful retention basis. Clearing history removes its known backup copies. Participation withdrawal and deletion of separately submitted examples are available in settings; intake requires separate consent and configured cleanup. Data already sent to AI is also governed by that service’s rules and deletion tools. If Drive has been used, clearing cloud data and deleting the planner account cover accessible data in the Drive app folder and earlier Firebase records. Completion requires valid access to both services in use. Retry after a network failure, revoked access, limit or error; queuing deletion locally does not mean every remote copy has been removed immediately. Disconnecting Drive on a device does not delete cloud records. Offline devices receive deletion markers on a later permitted connection; independently saved copies may require manual removal.

The application keeps up to 50 technical codes for the current session in RAM; the list disappears after closing. The automatic part of a report excludes tasks, conversations, audio, account details, keys, file paths and original error messages. You can add a description, review and save the report. Separate permission is required before opening an email. Recipient: lysokon995@gmail.com. You send the email through your own mail service, which adds the sender address and its own metadata. Do not include secrets or personal data in the description. Sent emails are stored in mailboxes under their rules; send deletion requests to the support address. The developer's code explanations do not constitute AI training.

Local reminders and interface tips

Reminders retain the mode, time and time zone selected on this device. When notifications are permitted, the operating system stores the schedule and display text. The daily “Waiting for reply” reminder does not contain task titles; an ordinary reminder for a particular task may show its title, including on the lock screen, according to OS settings. These settings are not sent to the developer and do not authorize experience collection. You can change or disable reminders in planner and system settings. The display, disabling and hiding of interface tips are saved only on the device for the corresponding profile; these actions are not sent as analytics.

Google Drive: sync behavior and limits

The planner accesses only its own hidden app folder through drive.appdata; access to all other Drive files is not requested. While the planner is open and sync is connected, it checks approximately every 30 seconds; manual sync and sending after saving are also available. Errors and limits increase the delay. Offline changes remain in a local queue when device storage is allowed. A closed app and OS restrictions can delay sync. API limits also apply to the shared app project; personal Drive storage does not remove shared quotas or guarantee free or instant delivery. Drive retention and storage locations are governed by Google and your account terms. End-to-end encryption is not implemented; Google may have technical access. Sync data is not copied to a separate developer server; legacy Firebase copies remain under the rules described above.
