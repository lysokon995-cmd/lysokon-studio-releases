Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-03.1. Contact: lysokon995@gmail.com.

Data used

The planner uses the tasks, dates, statuses and settings you enter. You choose the name and logo. Device assessment reads the CPU type and RAM amount to recommend a model; it does not collect files, contacts or a list of apps. Conversation history and confirmed memory are retained only by a separate choice. Google/Firebase Analytics SDKs and advertising identifiers are not integrated. Optional assistant-improvement examples and technical reports are described separately below.

Legal bases and separate permissions

Local features carry out your selected actions on entered records and settings to perform the agreement for use of the application. Where the law requires separate consent for additional processing, it is requested before the relevant feature. An account, selected synchronization categories, cloud AI, microphone access and improvement participation are not enabled by accepting the license once. Declining an optional feature does not block the basic planner.

Account and synchronization

Google sign-in uses the account identifier, account name/address for display and session tokens. Signing in alone does not enable content transfer. The separate “Connect the shared planner” action enables sync of tasks, retained conversations, confirmed memory and the planner title. Transfer stays off until connected; earlier refusals for individual sections are preserved until you explicitly connect the shared planner. Connecting does not authorize improvement examples, technical reports or text to be sent to cloud AI. Cloud Firestore content is configured for São Paulo; Firebase Authentication processes account data separately in the USA. The database region does not mean all provider metadata and operations remain in Brazil. There is no end-to-end encryption: the project administrator and Google may have technical access. Logos, models and AI keys are not synced. The app does not receive your Google password.

Cloud AI and voice

The selected AI receives your command and only context that you separately allow. Before sending, you select a specific service and model. Planner permissions do not change the service's contract, retention periods or account settings. Ordinary free Google APIs are unsuitable for personal, sensitive or confidential commands; real use is blocked without renewed confirmation of permitted use and a suitable billing project. Groq may retain abuse/reliability logs, generally for up to 30 days or longer where legally required, unless the relevant ZDR setting is enabled in the account. OpenRouter uses separate no-training and ZDR filters; these can reduce model availability and do not override legal exceptions, the service's own settings or separate safety retention. If a strict route is unavailable, a less private route is not selected automatically. Speech recognition can use operating-system/browser services and the network according to the platform and permissions; the application does not save audio recordings. Background activation and manual dictation are controlled separately.

Shared model improvement

Shared experience collection requires separate voluntary consent, experience-2026-10-31-v1, in Privacy. Accepting the terms, history, memory, sign-in and cloud AI do not replace it; the old installer checkbox does not authorize uploads. Each example is sent with a separate button: a user-reviewed, de-identified command/expected-result pair, model, language and date. Full task lists, history, audio, keys and files are not sent. The recipient is the Lysokon Studio developer, lysokon995@gmail.com; storage is Google Firebase, São Paulo. The purpose is evaluation and improvement of the planner assistant; it is not advertising or automatic training by third-party providers. The retention period is 90 days; hourly cleanup deletes expired examples, with possible delays if it fails. New uploads are blocked without connected cleanup and its recent confirmation. You can decline without limiting core features, withdraw consent and delete examples in settings. If the network disconnects, retry deletion: success is confirmed by checking that the collection is empty. A minimal disabled consent record remains to protect against resubmission. Sending an example does not change model weights; new recipients, purposes or data categories require new consent. Do not send personal data or secrets: automated checking does not replace human review. Examples are linked to your account UID for withdrawal and deletion. This is pseudonymized data, not a fully anonymous submission.

Technical connections and security

Update checks contact GitHub, model downloads contact the weight source, and selected cloud features contact the relevant service. Those services may see your IP address and technical connection metadata. Tasks are not included in update checks or installers. Keys are kept in the Mac/Windows system secure store; on a phone they exist only in the current session. Cloud-record access is restricted by account. No application guarantees absolute security.

Retention, withdrawal and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them. Turning sync off stops transfer but does not remove server records. Clearing cloud data and deleting the planner account are different actions. Full account deletion requires identity confirmation, successful content cleanup, deletion of the Firebase Authentication record and the account’s local profile on the device initiating deletion; it does not delete your Google account. Other installations may retain their own local copies even after receiving the server deletion marker; where needed, clear those copies separately on those devices. Before full deletion, the server block against re-enabling sync is checked. If strengthened server rules have not yet been applied, deletion stops with a technical code and the sign-in record is not reported as deleted. An error is not reported as completion. Independent original local records and files you export elsewhere are not deleted. Offline devices and data already sent to providers may require separate cleanup. Minimal service markers for disabling/deletion without task text remain to prevent restoration by stale devices; request their removal through support, subject to review of active sessions and the lawful retention basis. Clearing history removes its known backup copies. Participation withdrawal and deletion of separately submitted examples are available in settings; intake requires separate consent and configured cleanup. Data already sent to AI is also governed by that service’s rules and deletion tools.

Diagnostics and support

The application keeps up to 50 technical codes for the current session in RAM; the list disappears after closing. The automatic part of a report excludes tasks, conversations, audio, account details, keys, file paths and original error messages. You can add a description, review and save the report. Separate permission is required before opening an email. Recipient: lysokon995@gmail.com. You send the email through your own mail service, which adds the sender address and its own metadata. Do not include secrets or personal data in the description. Sent emails are stored in mailboxes under their rules; send deletion requests to the support address. The developer's code explanations do not constitute AI training.

Time zone and geolocation

The date and time come from the device clock. You can select a time zone manually, use the system zone, or determine it once by pressing a button with separate operating-system location permission. The application does not track continuously, store coordinates or send them to the developer; coordinates are converted to a time zone locally. Operating-system location services may use the network and process information under their own terms. Refusal does not prevent manual zone selection or working with tasks.

Voluntary technical testing

Separate consent, diagnostics-2026-10-31-v1, permits sending technical error codes, application version, platform and time to the developer's private testing center. Enabling it sends a test-session code, followed by up to 20 distinct codes per session. Original error messages, file paths, tasks, conversations, voice, keys, name, email and coordinates are not sent. Records are linked to the account UID to manage deletion. The recipient is Lysokon Studio, lysokon995@gmail.com; storage is Google Firebase, São Paulo, with a 30-day retention period and scheduled cleanup. Code summaries are sent to the developer's email; copies of emails are deleted upon a support request. You can decline without limiting the planner, stop sending and delete your server reports in settings. This is a separate purpose from collecting examples for AI improvement. Intake is blocked without recent cleanup confirmation. Server administration access is restricted to the owner's account.

Local reminders and interface tips

Reminders retain the mode, time and time zone selected on this device. When notifications are permitted, the operating system stores the schedule and display text. The daily “Waiting for reply” reminder does not contain task titles; an ordinary reminder for a particular task may show its title, including on the lock screen, according to OS settings. These settings are not sent to the developer and do not authorize experience collection. You can change or disable reminders in planner and system settings. The display, disabling and hiding of interface tips are saved only on the device for the corresponding profile; these actions are not sent as analytics.
